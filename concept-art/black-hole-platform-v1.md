## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: full-frame pixel-art environment concept for Surround, a contemplative Go game. Image only, no interface.
Primary request: an utterly spectacular celestial platform suspended near an immense black hole. Cosmic scale and awe, but a quiet place to sit and think.
Scene/backdrop: deep black-indigo space with an enormous perfectly dark black-hole silhouette dominating the upper half of the image. A richly textured amber, copper and pale-gold accretion disk sweeps horizontally around it; gravitational lensing curls the far side into majestic luminous arcs above and below the dark center. Dense ribbons of tiny distant stars bend around the silhouette. Very faint violet and midnight-blue interstellar dust gives the void depth. Make the black hole recognizably a black hole, not a planet, eclipse, magic portal or whirlpool.
Foreground: a broad ancient-futuristic celestial observation terrace floating in space, made of dark blue-gray stone and obsidian slabs with restrained fine brass inlays. A low elegant perimeter rim and a few small recessed cyan edge lights establish the platform's structure. The near terrace fills the bottom third, with generous EMPTY central floor space for a Go game interface to be added later. Uniform straight slab columns, evenly spaced seams and a perfectly level horizontal back edge. Correct one-point perspective with absolutely no tilted horizon or sideways-sloping floor. The camera is on the platform looking out, not an aerial view. Let the corners hint at monumental architecture without enclosing the view. A few distant tiny platform fragments can suggest vast depth; keep the foreground uncluttered.
Style/medium: exquisite detailed 16-bit/32-bit pixel art, hand-placed square pixel clusters, stepped silhouettes, textured dithering, rich pixel shading. The same premium retro adventure-game environment feel as a serene Japanese Go game's space expansion, not a glossy 3D render or a vector illustration.
Lighting/mood: amber disk light grazing the platform edges, cool blue reflected starlight across the stone. Epic, mysterious, impossibly tranquil. High visual drama through dark-to-warm contrast, NOT through blown-out brightness. Keep the luminous disk richly colored and comfortably exposed; avoid broad pure-white areas, blinding hotspots, lens flare or excessive bloom. The user found a visible Sun uncomfortable, so there is NO Sun in the frame.
Composition/framing: cinematic wide 16:9 landscape, full bleed, straight-on level camera. Clearly separate distant space, black-hole disk, and foreground platform for possible later layer animation. The black-hole structure remains well above the level platform horizon. Visually finished standalone scene, not a sheet of assets.
Constraints: no people, astronauts, Go boards, stones, tables, chairs, cushions, spacecraft, logos, text, labels, HUD, borders or watermark. No tilted platform, no irregular tile widths, no cluttered center.
