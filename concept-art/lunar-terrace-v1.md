## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: standalone pixel-art environment background for Surround, a contemplative Go game. Full image concept only, no interface.
Primary request: playing Go ON THE MOON, with both Earth and the Sun as the backdrop. An intentionally fantastical, breathtaking lunar study terrace: cosmic scale, profound quiet, a place to think.
Scene: we are standing on the lunar surface, not looking out from a spaceship. A simple broad, flat terrace made of pale gray lunar stone occupies the foreground and lower third, its edge merging into powdery silver regolith and scattered dark rocks. Keep this central playing area empty, uncluttered, absolutely level, with understated straight stone joints, NOT a Go-board grid. Beyond is an immense lunar crater basin with delicate craterlets, ridges and a jagged distant rim. No Earthlike atmosphere, blue sky, fog, ocean or plants on the Moon.
Backdrop: deep black-indigo space. A magnificent blue-and-white Earth hangs above the horizon toward the upper-left, large enough to appreciate oceans, continents and swirling cloud systems, with a thin cobalt atmospheric rim and a softly shaded night side. The Sun is distinctly visible toward the upper-right, a much smaller intensely bright ivory-gold disk with a restrained pixel-art halo. Earth’s illuminated side faces toward the Sun. A sparse scattering of tiny stars provides a stylized cosmic backdrop without becoming visual noise. Only one Earth and one Sun; no other planets.
Composition: wide cinematic 16:9 landscape. Viewed from a low, calm seated-eye-height perspective; a spacious clear middle and foreground will later accommodate a Go interface. Earth and Sun sit outside the central board area and remain striking at the sides. Broad low lunar horizon across the middle distance. Camera and terrace perfectly level; no slanted floor, no Dutch angle. Beautiful sense of silence and enormous distance, without cluttering the frame with machinery.
Light and color: lunar daytime, stark clean sunlight arriving from upper-right, bright silver illuminated rock faces and long crisp deep blue-violet shadows across the foreground. Earth provides the one rich sapphire/turquoise color accent; the Sun a small warm champagne accent. More luminous and inviting than horror or desolate sci-fi.
Style: exquisite high-detail PIXEL ART matching a premium retro Japanese Go/adventure game. Clearly visible crisp square pixel clusters, restrained dithering, finely textured rock and regolith, handmade-looking pixel Earth and clouds. Atmospheric in emotional mood but airless in physical appearance. Not a photo, not smooth 3D CGI, not blurry digital painting.
Constraints: one full-bleed image only. No people, astronauts, helmets, flags, spacecraft, train, buildings, pagodas, seats, Go boards, stones, game controls, text, logos or watermarks. Keep the foreground empty for gameplay. Do not turn the scene into a top-down map.
