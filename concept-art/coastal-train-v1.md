## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: standalone daytime pixel-art environment concept for Surround, a contemplative Japanese Go game. Full image only, no game interface.
Primary request: a creative new daytime setting — an empty vintage Japanese coastal train observation carriage on a radiant summer afternoon. The feeling of having the whole carriage to yourself, time to think between stops.
Scene/backdrop: inside a beautifully worn little seaside railway carriage, facing a wide bank of large panoramic windows across the side of the train. Cream-painted metal, honey-colored timber trim, brass luggage rails, a few hanging white hand straps near the upper edges. Built-in muted ocean-blue upholstered benches frame the left and right edges, leaving an open uncluttered wooden floor and generous space in the middle. Outside the windows: sparkling turquoise sea close to the railway, small green rocky islands, a distant quiet fishing village and layered blue coastal mountains, enormous white summer clouds in a vivid pale-blue sky. A slender strip of coastal grass at the bottom of the windows gives a near-distance layer. No identifiable real location required.
Composition/framing: elegant wide 16:9 landscape establishing shot, looking sideways toward the sea, NOT down a cramped aisle to a vanishing-point door. Beautiful view fills the upper-middle of the image, clear open floor across the lower portion. Frame most detailed carriage features near the left and right edges; central scenery calm enough for a future floating Go board interface, but do not draw that interface. Absolutely level camera, straight horizontal sea horizon and level floor, no Dutch angle. Natural, consistent perspective and evenly aligned floorboards. No large central table.
Lighting/mood: bright late-morning or early-afternoon daylight, warm rectangular window light on the floor and seats, cool teal interior shadows, a few delicate illuminated dust specks, brilliant little pixel glints on the sea. Airy, breezy, nostalgic, serene and quietly adventurous; not sunset, not night, not gloomy.
Style/medium: richly crafted crisp pixel art matching a premium retro Japanese adventure game, visible small square pixel clusters, controlled dithering, detailed fabric and wood textures, atmospheric depth. The warmth and stillness of a thoughtful Go anime translated into pixel art. Not photorealism, not smooth CGI, no painterly blur.
Constraints: one complete full-bleed environment image. No people, no Go board, no stones, no loose floor cushions, no text, no signs with lettering, no branding, no logo, no watermark, no UI, no collage. The empty carriage and outdoor scene should have clean separable silhouettes for possible future layered animation.
